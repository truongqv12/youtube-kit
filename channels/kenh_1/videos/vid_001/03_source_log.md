# 03 Source Log

## 1. 新潟市公式ウェブサイト (Niigata City Official Hub)
- **Role**: `official`
- **Link**: https://www.city.niigata.lg.jp/
- **Notes**: Validated senior hydration guidelines. Highlights that thirst sensors weaken with age and that morning hydration is critical to counter covert overnight fluid loss during sleep.

## 2. 日本農村医学会 (Japan Academy of Rural FNP)
- **Role**: `official`
- **Link**: https://www.jarfn.or.jp/
- **Notes**: Outlines risks and mechanisms of orthostatic hypotension in older adults, specifically delayed stabilization of blood pressure when transitioning from lying down to standing.

## 3. MSDマニュアル プロフェッショナル版 (MSD Manual)
- **Role**: `primary`
- **Link**: https://www.msdmanuals.com/ja-jp/
- **Notes**: Provides clear medical definitions on orthostatic hypotension mechanisms. Recommends positional transitions in stages (sitting before standing) as a core non-pharmacological intervention.

## 4. オムロン ヘルスケア (OMRON Healthcare)
- **Role**: `supporting`
- **Link**: https://www.omron.co.jp/
- **Notes**: Provides daily-life practical advice for seniors on morning routines. Explains the connection between drinking a glass of water upon waking and increasing circulating blood volume to suppress sudden blood pressure drops.

## 5. 笑顔ケア / 理学療法士監修記事 (Egao Care / PT Supervised Guides)
- **Role**: `supporting`
- **Link**: https://egaocare.net/
- **Notes**: Provides specific, safe, bed-bound stretching maneuvers for seniors (ankle rotations, \"グーパー\" toe clenches, and \"大の字\" star stretches) to safely ease morning joint stiffness and stimulate leg muscle pumps before standing.
