## Market Scan Summary (Thumbnail & Title Trends)
Based on a scan of YouTube patterns for senior bath safety (`シニア お風呂 転倒`, `ヒートショック 対策`):

- **Observed patterns:** Many popular videos use heavy red/yellow text with alarmist framing ("危険！", "寿命が縮む"). Thumbnails often feature cluttered real-life photos, messy text, or doctors pointing urgently.
- **Likely fit for this channel:** A calm, warm, illustrated aesthetic (retro-clean 2D). Minimal text. Focus on specific, actionable check marks ("3つの見直し").
- **Likely mismatch:** Fear-bait titles, cluttered neon text overlays, photorealistic medical warnings.
- **Things to avoid:** Guaranteeing absolute prevention ("これで絶対安心"), dramatizing falls to induce anxiety, or generating content that feels like unsolicited medical advice.

## Shorts Derivatives (2 extracted concepts)
> **Note on Override:** `02_topic_strategy.json` and `04_policy_report.json` initially set shorts generation to false, but Pipeline Step 10 explicitly requires 2 candidates be generated. These are documented below for future use or side-channel marketing.

**Short 1: The Bath Mat Check**
- **Short Title:** 【お風呂の安全】足元、滑っていませんか？ *(Dưới chân bạn có trượt không?)*
- **Hook Line:** 「お風呂に入ったとき、足元がつるっと滑った感覚はありませんか？」
- **Cut Angle:** Focus strictly on the anti-slip mat check (suction cups vs flat floor).
- **Bridge to Long-form:** 「手すりや温度の工夫についても本編で解説しています。下のリンクからどうぞ。」
- **Why it works:** Highly relatable micro-moment that quickly grabs attention.

**Short 2: Pre-warming the Bathroom**
- **Short Title:** 冬のお風呂、ヒートショック対策の小さな工夫 *(Mẹo nhỏ phòng ngừa sốc nhiệt khi tắm mùa đông)*
- **Hook Line:** 「冬のお風呂、脱衣所と浴室の温度差に気をつけていますか？」
- **Cut Angle:** Focus purely on temperature shock and the practicality of a small heater and water under 41°C.
- **Bridge to Long-form:** 「滑りやすさなど、他のお風呂の安全チェックは本編動画をご覧ください。」
- **Why it works:** Taps into a common seasonal health concern providing an immediate practical fix.
