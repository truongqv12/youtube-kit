# Source Log: vid_001 (Bath Safety)

## RQ1: Japanese senior bathroom falls statistics
- **Source 1**: Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁) guidance on senior fall prevention.
  - **Type**: `official`
  - **URL**: caa.go.jp
  - **Notes**: Highlights declining physical function and heat shock (blood pressure changes causing dizziness) as key triggers. Movements like standing up from the tub or walking on slippery floors are high risk.
- **Source 2**: Foundation of Socialistic Aging (長寿科学振興財団 - tyojyu.or.jp)
  - **Type**: `primary`
  - **URL**: tyojyu.or.jp
  - **Notes**: Advises water temp 41°C or less, 10 min baths, warming the bathroom to prevent sudden blood pressure changes that trigger falls.

## RQ2: Anti-slip flooring and mats evidence
- **Source 3**: Care equipment guides (roken.co.jp, xpert.link)
  - **Type**: `supporting`
  - **URL**: roken.co.jp / xpert.link
  - **Notes**: Anti-slip mats must match the floor type (suction cup for flat, self-sinking for uneven). They degrade over time and must be replaced. Shower chairs should not be placed over them.

## RQ3 & RQ6: Grab bars and Long-Term Care Insurance (介護保険制度)
- **Source 4**: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (厚生労働省 - mhlw.go.jp) & Municipal pages (e.g. Hioki City)
  - **Type**: `official`
  - **URL**: mhlw.go.jp / city.hioki.kagoshima.jp
  - **Notes**: Long-term care insurance (介護保険) covers grab bar installation and slip-resistant floor modification. Covers 70-90% of costs up to a 200,000 JPY limit. **Crucial rule**: Prior application is mandatory before construction starts.
- **Source 5**: General care providers (France Bed, Duskin)
  - **Type**: `supporting`
  - **URL**: francebed.co.jp
  - **Notes**: I-shape (vertical) is for standing/sitting. Horizontal is for moving. L-shape combines both. Average height is 800mm from floor, but must be adjusted to the individual.

## RQ4: Heat shock temperature management
- **Source 6**: Japan Association for Acute Medicine / Japan Circulation Society (via medical sites)
  - **Type**: `primary`
  - **URL**: jpnsh.jp / kyokaikenpo.or.jp
  - **Notes**: Emphasizes minimizing temperature difference between changing room and bathroom. Recommends water below 41°C.

## RQ5: Self-check checklists
- **Source 7**: Municipal safety checklists (Kurume City, Funabashi City)
  - **Type**: `official`
  - **URL**: city.kurume.fukuoka.jp / funabashi.lg.jp
  - **Notes**: Typical checks: steps at entrance, presence of grab bars, slip-resistant floors, temperature differences, and lighting. Recommends consulting Regional Comprehensive Support Center (地域包括支援センター).
