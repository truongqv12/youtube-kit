# Repetition and AI-Slop Checks

Flag if:
- 3 or more consecutive lines share the same sentence skeleton
- connectives are over-smoothed and repetitive
- the script explains without sounding observed or grounded
- many lines could be swapped with synonyms and keep the same rhythm
- every line lands with the same softener pattern
- the script sounds machine-translated, too formal, or too generically helpful
