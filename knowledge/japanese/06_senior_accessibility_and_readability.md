# Senior Accessibility and Readability

## Audience assumptions
- The target audience is older adults who benefit from calm pacing and familiar vocabulary.
- The channel should not talk down to viewers.
- The channel should avoid both youth slang and clinical coldness.

## Preferred wording traits
- familiar daily-life words
- concrete examples
- gentle reassurance
- practical verbs
- moderate sentence length
- respectful address without distance or stiffness

## Preferred framing
- `こういう方も多いです`
- `無理のない範囲で`
- `まずはここを見直してみましょう`
- `気になる場合は相談を`
- `すぐに変わらなくても大丈夫です`

## Avoid
- heavy jargon without explanation
- severe alarm framing
- moralizing tone
- expert-performance tone
- fake intimacy
- youth slang
- strong dialect performance unless explicitly targeted
