## Target Context
User MUST supply `TARGET_CHANNEL` (e.g. kenh_2) and `TARGET_VIDEO` (e.g. vid_001). Stop and ask if not provided.

- channels/{{TARGET_CHANNEL}}/videos/{{TARGET_VIDEO}}/03_research_brief.json
