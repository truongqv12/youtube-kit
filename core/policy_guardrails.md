# Policy Guardrails

- Avoid reused or overly repetitious content patterns.
- Avoid medical misinformation and unsupported health claims.
- Avoid diagnosis language unless the content is explicitly educational and carefully framed.
- Avoid cure, guaranteed prevention, medication-replacement, or anti-doctor framing.
- Avoid misleading visuals that appear as proof.
- Consider altered/synthetic disclosure if content appears realistic.
- Title and thumbnail must not overpromise.
- For early-phase channels in this niche, prefer daily-routine framing over disease-name shock framing.
- When symptoms, severe discomfort, or medication issues are mentioned, include soft consult-a-clinician framing.
- Calm caution is preferred over fear or urgency theater.
- Script wording should help understanding, not dramatize risk.
