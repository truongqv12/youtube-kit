# Title Thumbnail Psychology Standard

## Purpose

Create publish packaging that earns clicks through clarity, specificity, and self-relevance while preserving health trust.

## Title psychology angles

Each title candidate should explicitly use one primary angle:

- `number_anchor`: clear count such as 3 habits, 5 checks, 1 place to review.
- `specificity`: concrete age, timing, room, body action, or daily routine.
- `self_relevance`: speaks to what the viewer may notice in daily life.
- `mild_consequence`: bounded consequence without panic or certainty.
- `question_hook`: natural question older viewers may already have.

## Title rules

- Japanese publish title should be easy to parse at a glance.
- Prefer one clear promise over multiple stacked claims.
- Use `60代から`, `65歳から`, or daily-life anchors only when supported by the script.
- Do not imply diagnosis, guaranteed prevention, cure, reversal, or hidden truth.
- Avoid near-duplicate candidates with only minor word swaps.
- Market scan decides final length and pattern fit; do not lock by intuition alone.

## Thumbnail emotion spectrum

Choose one emotion level per video:

- `calm_informational`: neutral explainer, safest for trust.
- `gentle_concern`: mild concern visible in face/object/scene.
- `mild_alert`: stronger safety attention, still calm and practical.
- `strong_caution`: only for concrete safety topics; never panic or gore.

## Thumbnail rules

- Text is minimal, large, readable Japanese.
- Default maximum: 4 Japanese words unless publication profile is stricter.
- The visual should communicate the opening viewer question or safety concern.
- Host usage must match channel profile and make the host clearly 60+ when present.
- No fake before-after, medical certainty, body horror, emergency exaggeration, or copied competitor composition.

## Candidate evaluation

Each title and thumbnail candidate must include:

- `ctr_psychology_angle`.
- `viewer_question`.
- `script_alignment_reasoning`.
- `safety_rationale`.
- `ctr_prediction_reasoning` based on market scan patterns, not intuition alone.
